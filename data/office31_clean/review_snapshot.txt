REVIEW RULES
- Keep clearly identifiable examples of the intended class.
- Exclude incorrect labels and images outside the class definition.
- Exclude multiple main objects or composite product views.
- Small accessories may remain when the main object is clear.
- Keep one copy from each exact-duplicate group.
- Keep visually related product groups within the same split.
- Exclude unresolved class identities and document the uncertainty.
- Do not delete or rename original images.

9. LIMITATIONS AND NEXT STEPS

- The dataset is imbalanced, especially the bottle class.
- Product photographs may differ from real office camera images.
- Review decisions were made before training the new experiment.
- Visual grouping reduces overlap risk but cannot guarantee
  physical-object separation.
- Do not split related images independently.
- Apply augmentation only to training images.
- Choose model changes using validation results.
- Reserve the new test split for evaluation after model selection.
- Preserve the original pilot test result of 50% accuracy.
- The new experiment changes both the dataset and one class;
  its accuracy is not directly comparable with the original task.
- Record external provenance and check dataset usage terms
  before redistributing downloaded images.
